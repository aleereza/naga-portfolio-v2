# About Me

My love for photography began with two of my favourite things: travel and food. What started as a way to document the places I visited and the food I enjoyed gradually grew into a passion for capturing people, experiences, and the moments that tell a story.

Having lived in India, Australia, England, and Canada, and travelled to 27 countries and counting, I've been fortunate to experience different cultures and perspectives. These experiences have shaped how I see the world and what I look for through my camera.

Over the years, I've had the opportunity to photograph a wide variety of subjects, including portraits, weddings, family gatherings, corporate events, kids' and adult soccer leagues, swing dancing, theatre performances, pets, food, summer camps, and murals. I've also completed freelance photography assignments for Airbnb, capturing experiences and activities.

Travel and landscape photography remain close to my heart. Having spent several years living in Vancouver before moving to Toronto, I've developed a deep appreciation for Canada's natural beauty, from the mountains and coastlines of British Columbia to the changing seasons and landscapes of Ontario. I also enjoy wildlife, macro, and street photography, always looking for interesting details and perspectives along the way.

For me, photography is about more than just taking pictures. It's about capturing genuine moments, preserving memories, and telling stories through images. I also enjoy creating short video highlights that bring events and experiences to life.

I'm currently based in Toronto and available for photography assignments. Whether you're looking for portraits, event coverage, or have a creative project in mind, I'd love to hear from you.

Feel free to reach out at lightscameravancouver@gmail.com or follow my photography journey on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/candidreflectionsphotography/ "Instagram").

Thank you for stopping by!
